## Verified locally (real runs)
* `mvn verify` (Temurin 25.0.5, Spring Boot 4.1.2): **23 tests, 0 failures** — 8 pure domain + 15 against real `postgres:18.6` (including concurrent authorization and acknowledgement with one winner each, idempotent replay of recovery steps).
* `python tests/test_m40_openapi_contract.py`: 29 operations in parity with the Spring routes. `node --test tests/m40_reliability_frontend.test.mjs`: 9/9. `tsc`: no errors in M40 files (CSS-module declaration supplied by Next at build time).
* **Real backup/restore exercise** (`tools/m40_pg_backup_restore_exercise.sh`, PostgreSQL 18.6 in Docker, 50,000 synthetic rows, 2.5 MB custom-format dump): restored content digest equal to the source; a copy corrupted in the middle changed its SHA-256 and was refused or produced different content. Measured on this machine: backup 0.746 s, restore 0.630 s. **These are timings of one synthetic logical dump on a developer laptop; they say nothing about any hospital cell's RPO or RTO.**

## NOT verified / honest gaps
* **WAL archiving and point-in-time recovery were not exercised against a real server.** WAL continuity is verified over recorded LSN ranges (logic and database), not by replaying WAL. A real base-backup + archive + PITR exercise remains to be done in a disposable environment.
* **Interrupted recovery** is covered at the state-machine level (abort at any step, idempotent replays, optimistic versions), not by killing a real restore process.
* **Failed fencing** is covered as a refused step (egress and writers must both be confirmed); no real network egress is controlled by this service.
* No backup agent, no object-storage inventory scanner, no key-management integration (only key references are stored), no probe or collector: signals, backups and telemetry are submitted by authorised agents through the API.
* Redaction is deny-list and pattern based (keys such as name/email/token/note, e-mail addresses, bearer tokens, 7+ digit numbers). It is privacy-first but not a proof of absence of identifiers; free-text from untrusted sources needs review. Key matching is substring-based and may over-redact (for example host names).
* The reopening scope names services; actually re-enabling traffic is performed by external tooling.
* No outbox relay, no scheduler, no load test, no `next build`, no browser rendering, no accessibility audit, no dependency scan or SBOM. Java 27 lane is informational.

## Open integration dependencies
1. A backup agent that records backups (digest, WAL range, key reference, object references) and an independent verification job that recomputes digests and tests restores.
2. Collectors that submit signals and redacted telemetry with correlation and trace ids; OpenTelemetry export is not implemented here.
3. P03 grants for `m40:*` with separated maker/checker roles; infrastructure/network tooling to confirm fencing and reopen traffic.
4. Clinical and finance owners to define the clinical/financial reconciliation evidence required before reopening; governance for retention of telemetry and incident data.
5. A real PITR rehearsal and a measured RTO/RPO programme per cell before any target is published.
